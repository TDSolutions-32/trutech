Accessibility
statement.

We want everyone to be able to use our website, and we build our clients' sites with accessibility in mind.

Last updated September 26, 2026

Our commitment

Truly Digital Solutions works to make this website usable by as many people as possible, and aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

What we've done

  • Semantic headings, landmarks and a "skip to content" link for keyboard and screen-reader users.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Color contrast chosen for readability.
  • Motion and animation reduced automatically when your device's "reduce motion" setting is on.
  • A layout that adapts to phones, tablets and zoomed-in views.

Known limitations

Some older PDF case studies may not be fully accessible. We're happy to provide the same information in another format on request.

Tell us about a barrier

If anything on this site is hard to use, please email TDSolutions@trutech.us or call (561) 971-9512. Tell us the page and the problem, and we'll respond within 2 business days and work to fix it or provide the information another way.

Client websites

We build client sites with accessibility in mind. A formal WCAG conformance audit is available as a separate service.
