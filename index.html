Still curious? Ask us anything

Will you really design my site for free?

Yes. Every project starts with a free design demo so you're 100% happy with our work before you pay a dime. Once you approve the design and submit payment, we'll make any changes and complete your site.

What's included in a website care plan?

Every plan includes secure hosting and SSL, domain registration and renewal, weekly backups, software and security updates, uptime monitoring and monthly content edits — 30 minutes on Essential, 1 hour on Growth and 3 hours on Premium. Growth adds a monthly health report; Premium adds SEO and Google Business Profile updates and a quarterly speed and security review.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your business name and managed by us, so you always own it.

What platform will my new site be built on?

A professional website platform built for small business and nonprofit sites. We use Rackspace and AWS for reliable hosting, and every site includes an SSL certificate on a 256-bit encrypted HTTPS connection.

Will my website look good on smartphones?

Yes. Every site we design is fully responsive, automatically adjusting to desktops, laptops, tablets and phones.

Do you offer SEO?

Yes. SEO is part of every build — we optimize your content and structure to improve visibility and ranking in search results.

Can I keep my domain from my previous site?

Generally yes, as long as you still own it. Depending on your provider there may be a transfer process or fee — our team is happy to help.

Do you work with businesses outside Jacksonville?

Absolutely. We're based in Jacksonville, NC and happily work with clients all over the country by phone, email or virtual meeting.
