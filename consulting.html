FAQ

Consulting
questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us

Should I choose hourly or the retainer?

If you need help once in a while, hourly at $75 is simplest. If you expect to need 2 or more hours a month, or want priority access and a regular check-in, the $100 retainer is the better value.

What happens if I need more than 2 hours in a month?

Additional time is billed at our standard $75 hourly rate.

Do unused retainer hours roll over?

No. Your 2 retainer hours reset at the start of each month, and unused time doesn't carry over.

How do I cancel my retainer?

Give us 30 days' notice by phone at (561) 971-9512 or email at TDSolutions@trutech.us.

What if I need to cancel a session?

No problem. Just give us at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule, by phone at (561) 971-9512 or email at TDSolutions@trutech.us.

Is the intro call really free?

Yes. The 15-minute intro call is free with no obligation. We'll listen, answer questions and recommend a next step.

Do you work with clients outside Jacksonville?

Yes. We're based in Jacksonville, NC and work with clients all over the country by phone, email or video meeting.

Can consulting be combined with a website or care plan?

Absolutely. Many clients use consulting alongside a new website or one of our website care plans.
