FAQ

Commonly asked
questions.

Why should I use Truly Digital Solutions?

Maybe you built your own site and it didn't turn out how you hoped, or your site is outdated and you don't want to spend a lot. For us, quality and good service don't have to be expensive. We're committed to helping small businesses and have many happy clients.

Will you really design my site for free?

Yes. Every project starts with a free design demo so you're 100% happy before you pay a dime. Once you approve the design and submit payment, we'll make changes and complete your site.

What's included in a website care plan?

Every plan includes secure hosting and SSL, domain registration and renewal, weekly backups, software and security updates, uptime monitoring and monthly content edits. Plans start at $45/month — compare care plans.

What platform will my new site be built on?

A professional platform designed for small business and nonprofit websites, hosted on Rackspace and AWS with an SSL certificate on a 256-bit encrypted HTTPS connection.

Will my website look good on smartphones?

Yes. Every site is fully responsive and adjusts to desktops, laptops, tablets and smartphones.

Do you offer SEO?

Yes. We optimize your site's content and structure to improve visibility and ranking in search results.

Can I use my domain from my previous site?

Generally yes, as long as you still own it. There may be a transfer process or fee depending on your provider — we'll help.

Do you work with businesses outside Jacksonville?

Yes. We happily work with clients all over the country by phone, email or virtual meeting.
