The basics

What is
government
contracting?

Government contracting is how public agencies buy what they need from private businesses. Federal, state and local governments purchase everything from websites and IT support to construction, consulting and supplies — and they do it through formal, competitive contracts.

At the federal level, the process is governed by the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR), which sets the rules for how agencies publish opportunities, evaluate offers and award work. Contracts are posted publicly, businesses submit quotes or proposals, and the agency awards the contract to the offer that best meets its requirements.

To make sure small companies get a fair share, Congress sets small-business goals and agencies can "set aside" contracts so only qualifying small businesses may compete — including programs for veteran-owned, women-owned, HUBZone and 8(a) firms.

For a business like ours, it means we can partner directly with agencies — and with larger prime contractors — to deliver web, IT and cybersecurity work on public projects.

How it works

From registration
to award.

The federal path is standardized. Every vendor follows the same five steps.

  1. 01

    Register in SAM.gov

    Create an entity registration and receive a Unique Entity ID (UEI). Registration is free and renewed every year.

  2. 02

    Get a CAGE code

    The Defense Logistics Agency assigns a 5-character CAGE code that identifies the business to federal buyers.

  3. 03

    Choose NAICS codes

    Industry codes describe what you sell and decide which small-business size standards and set-asides apply.

  4. 04

    Find & bid

    Agencies post opportunities on SAM.gov. Businesses respond to requests for quotes (RFQs) and proposals (RFPs).

  5. 05

    Award & perform

    Win the award, deliver to the contract's terms and stay compliant — performance builds your past-performance record.

Know the language

Key terms, decoded.

SAM
System for Award Management
The official U.S. government system where businesses register to do business with federal agencies.
UEI
Unique Entity ID
A 12-character ID issued by SAM.gov. It replaced the DUNS number in 2022 as the government-wide vendor identifier.
CAGE
Commercial & Government Entity code
A 5-character identifier assigned by the Defense Logistics Agency to registered vendors.
NAICS
North American Industry Classification System
6-digit codes that classify a business's industry and determine small-business size standards.
Set-aside
Restricted competition
A contract reserved for small businesses or a specific socio-economic program so only they can compete.
FAR
Federal Acquisition Regulation
The primary rulebook governing how executive agencies buy goods and services.
Cap Statement
Capability statement
A one-page résumé for government buyers: core competencies, past performance, codes and contact info.
CMMC
Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification
The Department of Defense's cybersecurity standard for contractors that handle sensitive information.
